**QWebMap 4.38.8**

QWebMap started as a fork of [GallPeters/QGIS2VectorTiles](https://github.com/GallPeters/QGIS2VectorTiles) by Jossef Kanter. It is not an official release of the original plugin, and it installs next to it.

This is the **generic** edition — the one to download. (A special Hungarian zoning-plan edition, not for general use, is released separately with a `-hu` tag.)


### Improved
- **Parcel report print:**
  - The opened full regulation texts run one after the other across the whole width in three columns (two on portrait paper), without page breaks between zones, so no paper is left blank.
  - A section shared by several zones (between horizontal rules, such as the general rules) is printed once; at the other zones a line says it is the same as printed above.
  - The parts and restrictions beside the map are compact rows (no share bars or cards), so they fit on the first page.
