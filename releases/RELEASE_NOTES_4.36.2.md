**QWebMap 4.36.2**

QWebMap started as a fork of [GallPeters/QGIS2VectorTiles](https://github.com/GallPeters/QGIS2VectorTiles) by Jossef Kanter. It is not an official release of the original plugin, and it installs next to it.

This is the **generic** edition — the one to download. (A special Hungarian zoning-plan edition, not for general use, is released separately with a `-hu` tag.)

### Zoomed-out load
- **Long layer names**: a very long name widened the progress window and pushed it to the right. Names are now shortened with "…" (the progress window keeps a fixed width; in the table the whole name is in the tooltip and the search still finds it), and the result window is centred over the Publish window.
- **Tick boxes**: their tooltip explains them — ticked, the zoom is applied with the button; they are ticked by themselves where a change is suggested, and unticked the zoom shown is the current one.
