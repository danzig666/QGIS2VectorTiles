**QWebMap 4.38.7**

QWebMap started as a fork of [GallPeters/QGIS2VectorTiles](https://github.com/GallPeters/QGIS2VectorTiles) by Jossef Kanter. It is not an official release of the original plugin, and it installs next to it.

This is the **generic** edition — the one to download. (A special Hungarian zoning-plan edition, not for general use, is released separately with a `-hu` tag.)


### Improved
- **Parcel report print:** the first page has the map with the parcel and its parts beside it. The restrictions follow from the next page, then each opened full regulation text from a new page, across the whole width in two columns (before, everything after the first page ran on in the narrow column beside the map, leaving most of each page empty). Headings stay with the text that follows, and restriction cards and list items are not split across pages. Closed full texts are not printed.
