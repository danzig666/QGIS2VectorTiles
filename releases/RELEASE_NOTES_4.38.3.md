**QWebMap 4.38.3**

QWebMap started as a fork of [GallPeters/QGIS2VectorTiles](https://github.com/GallPeters/QGIS2VectorTiles) by Jossef Kanter. It is not an official release of the original plugin, and it installs next to it.

This is the **generic** edition — the one to download. (A special Hungarian zoning-plan edition, not for general use, is released separately with a `-hu` tag.)

### Web map
- **Search as a magnifier on phones**: on a phone (or any window up to 760 px wide) the search field took a whole row under the title, covering map. It is now a magnifier button at the end of the title bar. Tapping it opens the search field with the keyboard. The field closes again when a result is chosen (the magnifier is then highlighted to show a search is set; tap it to see or change it), with Escape, or when it is left empty. Wider screens keep the search field as before. A viewer change: republish for it to take effect.
