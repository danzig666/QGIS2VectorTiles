**QWebMap 4.40.1**

QWebMap started as a fork of [GallPeters/QGIS2VectorTiles](https://github.com/GallPeters/QGIS2VectorTiles) by Jossef Kanter. It is not an official release of the original plugin, and it installs next to it.

This is the **generic** edition — the one to download. (A special Hungarian zoning-plan edition, not for general use, is released separately with a `-hu` tag.)


### Added
- **Large reading window for long texts:** a zone's full regulations (parcel report and zone popups) have an *Open in a large window* button. The window shows the text at a readable width over the map, with larger or smaller type (A− / A+). From there **Print** prints the text alone (with its title and the map's name, not the map page) and **Save** downloads it as a standalone HTML file. Escape or a click beside the window closes it.
