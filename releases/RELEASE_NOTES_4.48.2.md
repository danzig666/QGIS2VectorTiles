**QWebMap 4.48.2**

QWebMap started as a fork of [GallPeters/QGIS2VectorTiles](https://github.com/GallPeters/QGIS2VectorTiles) by Jossef Kanter. It is not an official release of the original plugin, and it installs next to it.

This is the **generic** edition — the one to download. (A special Hungarian zoning-plan edition, not for general use, is released separately with a `-hu` tag.)

### Changed
- **Search behind its magnifier on every screen**: the web map shows only a magnifier in the header; the search field opens when it is clicked and closes again when left empty, with Escape, or once a result is chosen. Until now this was so only on phones; wider screens always showed the field.
