v4.19.0
08 Oct 2026
Temporary (memory) layers, e.g. kept by the Memory Layer Saver plugin, are published with their features (they came out empty: "not visible at this zoom").
The web map stays on the publication's extent: no panning away, and it zooms out only a little beyond the whole extent (option, on by default).
v4.18.0
07 Oct 2026
Raster layers are published as WebP images by default: smaller than PNG and still transparent.
New "Publish the visible layers" button: the published layers become the ones visible in the QGIS Layers panel.
v4.17.0
07 Oct 2026
Point patterns drawn as whole markers in QGIS (e.g. "Cross-Stitch") are whole markers in the browser too, on QGIS's grid and stacked in QGIS's drawing order, without seams or bands.
Point patterns clipped to the shape use QGIS's own texture, so overlapping markers stack as in QGIS.
Markers sized in pixels are no longer half-size on high-resolution screens.
v4.16.0
07 Oct 2026
Pattern fills start exactly where QGIS starts them: line, point and SVG patterns at each feature's bottom-left corner, raster fills at each part's top-left ("Align pattern to: Feature"), to the pixel.
v4.15.0
07 Oct 2026
Arrows are the exact shapes QGIS draws (straight, curved, repeated, half arrows), filled with their own fill symbol; drop shadows fall on earlier arrows as in QGIS.
Inner shadow and inner glow on lines are shaded like QGIS (e.g. "effect emboss"); outer glows and drop shadows no longer show spikes on lines with many short segments.
Line offsets in millimetres or pixels follow QGIS's offset line at sharp corners instead of looping (e.g. "topo steps"); simple fill offsets are applied.
v4.14.1
07 Oct 2026
Pattern fills set to "Align pattern to: Viewport" (raster fills: "Coordinate mode: Viewport") start at the corner of the map view, exactly as in QGIS.
v4.14.0
07 Oct 2026
Every built-in QGIS symbol type on the web: lineburst, interpolated and raster image lines, vector field markers, merged features, inverted polygons, heatmaps, point cluster and point displacement renderers.
Pattern fills keep their QGIS size at every zoom and are pixel-sharp; gradients are smooth (no stripes); arrows taper and keep their drop shadow; screen-size marker intervals stay within 4 % of QGIS.

Publish window: the Scales column shows the layer's QGIS scale range, e.g. "(QGIS 1:2 000 –)".

Optional Google Street View in the web map (your API key): blue lines show where it exists, a tap opens the panorama looking toward the tapped spot.

v4.12.0
04 Oct 2026
Raster layers are reused from the export cache while the raster, its style and its export settings are unchanged.
Raster tiles are rendered and encoded on several CPU cores (2.4× faster on 4 cores), with identical output.
A parcel report prints zoomed on the parcel, at the closest standard scale that shows it (down to 1:100).
v4.11.1

Free (angled) polygon labels turn like in QGIS (its labeling engine's rule, measured font widths); with "Centroid: whole polygon" they were drawn horizontally.
v4.11.0
04 Oct 2026

SVG fills without white seams between tiles, rotated SVG fills without breaks; legend swatches use legend patch shapes.
Map themes: "Publish its layers" adds layers and never unpublishes, so several themes add up.
v4.10.0
04 Oct 2026
Gradient fills (linear, radial, conical; pad, reflect, repeat; two colours or a colour ramp) and shapeburst fills are drawn on the web as fine colour bands, about 1 colour level from QGIS on average.
Outer glow and drop shadow effects on lines are drawn as blurred line layers; marker effects are no longer falsely reported as ignored.
Arrow lines take the colour of their top visible fill; new QWebMap icon.

v4.1

Release of the fork danzig666/QGIS2VectorTiles; installs as QGIS2VectorTiles (fork) next to the official plugin.

v4.0
30 Sep 2026
Release of the fork danzig666/QGIS2VectorTiles, based on v3.6.
Much closer match between the QGIS map and the web map, checked symbol by symbol against QGIS renders of real style libraries.
Marker lines are placed exactly where QGIS draws them: interval, vertex, centre and segment markers, offset along the line, averaged angles at corners, ring filters and polygon outline offsets; markers no longer go missing at tile edges.
Point pattern, line pattern (hatch), SVG, raster image and random marker fills keep the QGIS spacing, density and clipping; large map-unit patterns are drawn as real geometry.
Map-unit dashes, Qt dash patterns and caps, sub-pixel line widths, font markers and geometry generators follow QGIS.
Map-unit line labels and label backgrounds are drawn at every zoom; pinned labels and callouts are supported.
Sharper map-unit markers at every zoom, and a sprite sheet that stays within browser texture limits.
Large and detailed layers export much faster: big polygons are processed in pieces, and very dense patterns fall back to textures.
A fidelity report lists every symbol part that is approximated or not supported; strict mode stops the export on fidelity errors.
Optional static web package with relative URLs that runs from any web server folder.
